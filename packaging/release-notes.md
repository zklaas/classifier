## Download

| Your computer | Download | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Linux** (64-bit PC) | [Classifier-Linux-x86_64.AppImage](https://github.com/zklaas/classifier/releases/latest/download/Classifier-Linux-x86_64.AppImage) | Right-click it > *Properties* > *Allow executing as program* (or `chmod +x` it), then double-click. |

Nothing else is needed: Python and everything Classifier uses are inside.

### The first time you open it

* **Linux** asks nothing, once the file is allowed to run.
